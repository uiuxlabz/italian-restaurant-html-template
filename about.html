Our Story

From a Tuscan kitchen to your neighborhood — the journey of Trattoria Bella.

The Beginning

A Dream Born in Tuscany

In 1998, Chef Marco Bellini left his family's agriturismo in the rolling hills of Tuscany to pursue a dream: bringing the authentic flavors of his homeland to a new neighborhood in New York City. With recipes passed down through four generations and a suitcase full of handmade pasta cutters, he opened the doors of Trattoria Bella in a tiny storefront on Via Roma.

What began as a 12-seat kitchen quickly grew into a beloved gathering place. Word spread about the handmade tagliatelle, the wood-fired Margherita, and the warmth of a dining room that felt like sitting at Marco's family table.

Today, nearly three decades later, Trattoria Bella remains committed to the same philosophy: honest ingredients, simple preparations, and the belief that a great meal is an act of love.

Milestones

Through the Years

1998

The First Flame

Chef Marco opens Trattoria Bella with 12 seats, a wood-fired oven, and a menu of six classic dishes.

2004

Expansion

The restaurant expands to 60 seats, adding a private dining room and an expanded wine cellar featuring over 200 Italian labels.

2010

Award-Winning

Trattoria Bella receives the City's Best Italian Restaurant award and is featured in Food & Wine magazine.

2016

The Garden

Opening of the secret garden patio — an intimate outdoor dining space with climbing vines and string lights.

2023

Next Generation

Marco's daughter Sofia joins as head pastry chef, introducing a beloved dessert menu that honors tradition with a modern touch.

The Team

Meet Our Chefs

The passionate people behind every dish — each bringing their own story and expertise to our kitchen.

Marco Bellini

Founder & Executive Chef

Born in Tuscany, trained in Bologna. Marco's philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, honor the recipe, and cook with heart.

Sofia Bellini

Pastry Chef

Marco's daughter, trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris. Sofia brings a modern European finesse to classic Italian desserts.

Antonio Russo

Sous Chef

From Naples, Antonio is the master of our wood-fired oven. His Neapolitan pizza technique has been honed over 15 years.

Elena Marchetti

Pasta Artisan

Elena hand-rolls every strand of pasta each morning. Her tagliatelle and ravioli are the heart of our kitchen.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Authentic Ingredients

We import olive oil from Tuscany, San Marzano tomatoes from Campania, and Parmigiano-Reggiano aged 24 months from Emilia-Romagna. Every ingredient tells a story.

Handmade with Care

Every pasta shape is cut by hand every morning. Our pizza dough ferments for 72 hours. We believe the best food takes time, patience, and love.

Community & Family

Trattoria Bella is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place. From birthday celebrations to Tuesday night dinners, we treat every guest like family.

Sustainability

We source locally whenever possible, compost all kitchen waste, and partner with nearby farms. Good food starts with a healthy planet.
